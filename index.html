Rupanshu Shah

PhD Scholar, Computer Science and Engineering
Indian Institute of Technology Delhi

I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Delhi, advised by Prof. Rajendra Kumar. I work on quantum algorithms and fine-grained complexity.

My research studies classical computational problems through a quantum lens, primarily problems in P, with two complementary aims: designing the best possible quantum algorithms for them, and proving quantum lower bounds that show where such speedups stop. Much of my current work is on the quantum complexity of All-Pairs Shortest Paths (APSP) and its fine-grained relatives.

Before my PhD, I spent a year as a Predoctoral Fellow at IIT Delhi working on instance compression. I received my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from IIT Delhi in 2023, with a department specialization in Theoretical Computer Science.
